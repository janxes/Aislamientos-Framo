## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a reliable, professional approach tailored for insulation and energy-efficiency services in Madrid. Built to align seamlessly with WordPress Gutenberg (TT5) architectures, it pairs authoritative editorial typography with clean, utilitarian structure. The aesthetic tone is anchored by a warm, inviting surface, reassuring homeowners and commercial partners alike of technical competence and regional expertise.

## Colors

The color architecture directly mirrors the brand's core visual identity derived from its technical scope. The seed color (`#2C3539`) acts as the primary charcoal/slate grey for structural elements and roofs, while secondary energy red symbolizes solar warmth and thermal efficiency. Clean sky blue serves as a tertiary accent representing cooling, and a warm, inviting surface background (`#FBF9F6`) delivers a comfortable, human-centric reading environment.

## Typography

Typography balances literary authority with clean legibility. Headlines utilize **Newsreader**, providing a distinguished, trustworthy editorial voice well-suited for high-end residential and commercial services. Body copy and technical metrics rely on **Source Sans 3**, ensuring pristine readability across documentation, service descriptions, and Gutenberg block layouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid, content-first grid optimized for WordPress Gutenberg patterns. Spacing tokens enforce a reliable 8px rhythm, scaling comfortably from dense mobile viewports to expansive desktop displays. Generous vertical padding (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) between sections establishes a calm, professional cadence appropriate for architectural and engineering services.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on a combination of tonal layering against the warm `#FBF9F6` surface and low-contrast outlines. Avoid heavy drop shadows; instead, use subtle surface containers in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with delicate borders to demarcate interactive cards, testimonials, and quote calculators. This approach maintains a clean, modern aesthetic rooted in tactile reliability.

## Shapes

With a roundedness level of `1` (Soft), UI elements feature a gentle `0.25rem` radius for standard components, scaling to `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) for cards and containers. This slight softening prevents the layout from feeling overly industrial while preserving the precise, professional geometry expected of a premier Madrid contracting service.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid charcoal (`#2C3539`) background with white text and soft rounded corners. On hover, shifts to energy red (`#D32F2F`) for primary calls-to-action like "¡Me interesa!".
- **Secondary:** Outlined with charcoal borders on warm surface backgrounds, shifting to solid tint on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Utilized for location tags (e.g., "Aluche", "Barajas") and compliance callouts ("NextGenerationEU"). Styled with compact padding, subtle neutral backgrounds, and label typography.

### Input Fields & Forms
- Clean border-led text fields featuring 100% full-width mobile responsiveness, clear placeholder states in muted slate, and high-contrast focus rings in cooling blue (`#1E73BE`).

### Cards
- Service and testimonial cards use pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) set against the warm background, anchored by a subtle outline and Newsreader section headers.

### Additional Components
- **Direct Action Bar:** Fixed mobile footer component housing a direct telephone link and WhatsApp integration button for instant conversions.
